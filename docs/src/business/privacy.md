# Privacy for Business

Recode Business provides enterprise-grade data privacy protections for teams and organizations.

## Enforced Protections

For all members of a Recode Business organization:

- **No prompt sharing:** Conversations and prompts are never shared with Recode.
- **No training data sharing:** Code context is never shared with Recode for
  model training or fine-tuning.
- **Local-first guarantee:** Your codebase stays strictly within your company's boundary.

## AI Privacy Controls

These controls cover what Recode stores and processes:

- **Bring your own keys:** API requests go directly from your local machines to model providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Vertex, etc.).
- **Zero data retention:** Recode operates with zero data retention commitments on all model communications.
- **Local offline models:** Organizations can mandate local offline inference (e.g. via Ollama), guaranteeing zero bytes leave the workstation.
