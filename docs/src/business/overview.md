# Recode Business

Recode Business is Recode designed for engineering teams and enterprises. Members with a paid Business seat receive access to Recode's hosted AI models, local AI agent orchestration, and unlimited Edit Predictions. Administrators control which AI capabilities are active, what data leaves your environment, and how AI spend is allocated.

It is purpose-built for teams that demand maximum AI productivity without security or compliance compromises.

## What's included

Every member gets access to all [hosted AI models](../account/recode-hosted-models.md) and [Edit Predictions](../ai/edit-prediction.md).

For the organization:

- **Enforced data controls:** Administrators configure AI and data settings for the whole organization from the Data & Privacy dashboard. Controls include the [Recode Model Provider](./admin-controls.md#hosted-ai-models), [Edit Predictions](./admin-controls.md#edit-predictions), and feedback controls. Members cannot override these settings individually.
- **Private by default:** Recode never stores your prompts, logs code snippets, or trains foundation models on your intellectual property. All telemetry can be disabled org-wide with a single toggle.
- **[Roles and permissions](../roles.md):** Owners, admins, Billing Managers, and members have distinct, secure access levels.
- **Consolidated billing:** Your team's licenses and AI usage appear on [one invoice](../account/billing.md#organization), with no separate bills per member.
