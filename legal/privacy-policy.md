# Recode Privacy Policy

**Last Updated**: November 2026

Recode respects your privacy and is designed with privacy-first principles.

## 1. Local-First Processing
Recode is a local-first editor. Your source code, local files, and workspace contents remain on your computer and are never uploaded to any central server by Recode.

## 2. Telemetry and Diagnostics
You have complete control over editor telemetry and crash reporting. All telemetry can be toggled on or off at any time in Settings. Telemetry, when enabled, collects anonymous usage metrics to improve editor stability.

## 3. Third-Party Services and AI
When you use AI integrations (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or Ollama), prompts and completions are communicated directly between your local editor and the configured API endpoints using your provided credentials. Recode does not intercept, log, or train on your private code.
